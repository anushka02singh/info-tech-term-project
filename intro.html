Information Technology in Libraries

This section of the website explores how modern information technology is shaping the way libraries function, serve communities, and share information. From open-source tools to specialized information systems, technology plays a major role in access, organization, and digital services.

What You Will Explore

Throughout the Info Tech section, you will be able to:

  • Learn about open-source technology and how it is used in library environments
  • Participate in interactive surveys to share your opinions
  • Explore the information tools covered in this course
  • View real-time survey results stored in a database

Each page in this section is designed to demonstrate both technical understanding and real-world application of information technologies used in libraries today.
