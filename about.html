About Me

Introduction

Hello! My name is Anushka, and I'm a part-time graduate student currently pursuing a course in Information Technology. This project is the culmination of my learning journey, where I combine the technical skills gained throughout the semester, including HTML5, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL, to create a functional and aesthetic website.

Through this project, I wanted to explore how technology can create personalized and engaging user experiences. The site you see here is a product of my passion for both web development and my interest in romantic comedies.

Fun Facts

  • I'm a big fan of romantic comedies, especially from the late '90s and early '00s.
  • I have a hobby of creating small websites and experimenting with different design styles.
  • When I'm not coding, you can find me binge-watching rom-coms or reading romance novels.
